Drag one department onto another to swap positions. Alternatively, click two departments to swap them. The main aisle remains fixed.

2. Edit material flows

Each row represents one directed movement. Reverse trips can be entered separately; travel distance is symmetric.

Default route: R–C–M–B–M–I–P–R. Each movement has 10 loads/day.

3. Travel distance model

Each bay is 8 m wide and 8 m deep. The 2 m main aisle runs between the two rows. Department centers are used as reference points.

All different-department trips: d = 5 + 8 × |column difference| + 5 m, including trips within the same row. The 5 m represents 4 m from the department center to the aisle plus 1 m to its centerline on each side.
